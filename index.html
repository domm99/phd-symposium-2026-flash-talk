Self-Organizing Federated Learning

From one fragile global model to local, adaptive self-federations
Davide Domini
3rd year PhD student
davide.domini@unibo.it
Reference scenario

Learning at the edge, where context matters

Nearby devices observe related phenomena, but the whole system remains spatially heterogeneous.

Challenges

why hard
  • 1Non-IID data breaks the assumption behind one shared global model.
  • 2Central aggregation becomes a bottleneck and a single point of failure.
  • 3Edge devices are resource-constrained, mobile, and intermittently available.

Research direction

my PhD

Use field-based coordination to let devices discover who to learn with, then train specialized and lightweight models.

Spatial context Local observations plus neighbor interactions.
Self-federations Emergent leaders and adaptive regions.
Specialized models Sparse, personalized training at the edge.
